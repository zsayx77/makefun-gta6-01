Velvet Bay — Street Heat is a top-down open-world action game: ten story missions — the finale is The Vault, a bank heist where you hold Bay National for 30 seconds, grab $500, and escape to Sunset Garage on the crew's route — five-star police chases with helicopters, a living coastal city with a day/night cycle, a three-person crew with real bonuses, a live HUD minimap with a legend, and pistol combat. No build step, no network — just open it and drive.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in your browser.

## How to play

- WASD / arrows — move and drive, SHIFT to run
- E — steal or exit a car
- SPACE — punch
- Mouse — aim the pistol, click to fire (unlocks after mission 5)
- P — pause
